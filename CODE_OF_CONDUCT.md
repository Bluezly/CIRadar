# Code of Conduct

CI Radar aims to keep technical discussion professional, specific, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Critique code, designs, and claims rather than people.
- Give enough technical detail for others to understand and reproduce a concern.
- Respect privacy and remove confidential information from public discussions.
- Accept correction and update claims when evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, sustained personal attacks, or disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use public issues to disclose private information about another person.
